ABOUT

Our tea house welcomes guests with an inviting
ambiance that combines rustic charm and modern
aesthetics.The walls are adorned with local artwork,
creating a vibrant atmosphere that reflects the rich
culture of Tamil Nadu. We offer an assortment of teas,
from classic masala chai to refreshing green tea, with
each cup brewed to perfection.

Our menu also features blends infused with local
spices and herbs, highlighting the flavors of the region.
Guests can enjoy their tea in our cozy seating area,
surrounded by lush greenery, or take advantage of our
outdoor space, perfect for rainy afternoons.
